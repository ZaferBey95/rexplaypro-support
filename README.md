## FAQ

**Q: Does this app provide content?**
A: No. REX PLAY PRO is a media player only. You need your own streaming service credentials.

**Q: Which player engines are supported?**
A: VLC and AVPlayer.

**Q: How do I remove ads?**
A: Go to Settings → Remove Ads to purchase the ad-free version.
