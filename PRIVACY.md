# REX PLAY PRO - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

## Overview
REX PLAY PRO is a media player application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

## Data Collection
This app does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data with third parties.

## What the app stores locally on your device:
- Login credentials (server address, username, password) — stored locally only
- App settings and preferences
- Favorites and watch history
- PIN code for parental controls

All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

## Third-Party Services
- **Google AdMob**: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Content
This app does not provide or host any content. Users connect to their own third-party streaming services.
